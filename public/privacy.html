Rhythm Relay

Your practice, your data.

Privacy notice for Rhythm Relay, a browser-based music practice app operated by Koa Chang.

Updated September 16, 2026 (UTC)

Development status: Sign-in, new-project export, and saved-note verification have succeeded with the app owner's Audiotool account. Access from another account has not yet been verified. You can practice without connecting an account.

Practicing in your browser

Rhythms, tap timings, and practice results are processed in your browser. Tap timings and results stay in memory and reset when you reload or close the page. They are not sent to the operator or to Audiotool.

The app synthesizes its practice sounds. It does not use your microphone, record audio, or upload audio samples. Rhythm Relay has no analytics, advertising, or operator-run backend, and the operator does not receive your sign-in tokens.

Connecting to Audiotool

Connecting is optional. The official Audiotool SDK handles sign-in directly with Audiotool, requests the project:write permission, and fetches your account username to show your connection status. Your Audiotool password is entered on Audiotool’s service.

When you choose to export, the app sends the project title, tempo, and generated drum notes to Audiotool. It creates a new project, then reopens that same project to check the saved rhythm. The app does not list or read your other projects, and does not modify them. The requested permission is broader than these actions; review Audiotool’s authorization screen before connecting.

What stays in browser storage

The app saves its public Audiotool client ID in local storage. The SDK stores access and refresh tokens, their expiry, and temporary sign-in verification data there so it can complete sign-in and reconnect. This data remains in your browser until cleared by the SDK or through your browser’s site-data controls.

Disconnect this browser clears the SDK’s local sign-in data and reloads the page. It does not revoke Audiotool’s server-side authorization or delete exported projects. Clearing this site’s browser data also removes the saved client ID. Manage retained projects and account permissions through Audiotool; its own policies govern data held there.

Services your browser contacts

The published site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The practice page loads fonts from Google Fonts, and connecting or exporting makes requests to Audiotool. These services receive normal connection information, such as your IP address and browser information, when your browser contacts them. Their handling and retention of that information is described in the GitHub privacy statement, Google privacy policy, and Audiotool privacy policy.

This privacy page itself loads no external fonts or scripts.

Audience and questions

Rhythm Relay is general music practice for people aged 13 and older; it is not directed at children under 13. Audiotool has its own account eligibility rules.

For questions about this app, contact Koa Chang through the Rhythm Relay issue tracker. Issues are public: do not post passwords, sign-in tokens, or private account information. Information you choose to post there is visible to the operator and other GitHub users.

This notice describes the current app. Changes to its data handling will be reflected here with an updated date.
